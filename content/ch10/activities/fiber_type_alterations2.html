🔬 Molecular Mechanism & Histochemical Rationale: MHC-IIx to MHC-IIa Compression

Regular high-intensity resistance training forces high-frequency motor unit recruitment, which systematically represses transcription of the erratic, fatigable pure MHC-IIx gene. The fiber pool shifts toward highly fatigue-resistant, hypertrophied Type IIa fibers, collapsing the uncommitted hybrid pool.
