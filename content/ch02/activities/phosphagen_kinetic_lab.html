Exercise Physiology Insight

Why does intramuscular ATP concentration stay almost flat while CP plummets?

The ATP Buffering Paradox

Resting muscle only has enough free ATP for 1–2 seconds of maximal work. Creatine kinase re-phosphorylates ADP so rapidly that ATP drops less than 20–25%, preventing early cross-bridge rigor.

The 10-Second Coaching Rule

Between 5–10s, CP drops by 50–70%. By 10–12s, with CP depleted >85%, cross-bridge velocity drops noticeably, forcing rapid reliance on anaerobic glycolysis (producing H⁺ and lactate).

Program Design Prescription To target pure neuromuscular explosive power without inducing acidosis, keep work bouts strictly ≤ 10 seconds with full aerobic rest (2–5 minutes) to resynthesize CP stores.
